1401 PROBE · HARDWARE SCANNER · HOW TO USE IT

1401 Probe scans your computer's hardware from a USB stick and saves one report. You upload the report at nullmothsystems.com, and that tells us exactly what your hardware needs. It takes about 15 minutes.

You need: a USB stick of 1 GB or more that you don't need anything on (it gets erased), and this PC.

1 · Make the stick (in Windows)

  1. Open 1401 Probe and click Make the scanner USB stick.
  2. The app checks this PC. If Secure Boot is on, you'll turn it off in step 2. If BitLocker is on, click Pause BitLocker for 3 restarts. Otherwise Windows will ask for your recovery key after you change Secure Boot.
  3. Plug in the USB stick, pick it in the list, tick the box, and click Erase and make the stick. Only USB drives are listed. Your internal drives are never shown.

2 · Start the scanner

  1. If Secure Boot is on: click Restart into BIOS setup, find Secure Boot (usually under Boot or Security), set it to Disabled, then save and exit. The scanner isn't signed by Microsoft, so it can't start while Secure Boot is on.
  2. Leave the stick plugged in. Press the boot-menu key as the PC starts, and pick the USB stick marked UEFI. You can also click Restart now and pick the USB stick in the app and choose Use a device.
    ASUS F8MSI F11Gigabyte F12ASRock F11
    Dell F12HP F9Lenovo F12Acer F12
  3. Choose 1401 Probe - scan this computer (it starts by itself after 5 seconds).

3 · Let it scan

  • The scan takes about 2 to 8 minutes. The last step is a graphics test. The screen may flicker or go black for up to 3 minutes. That's normal, so leave it running.
  • When it says DONE, the report is saved on the stick and the computer turns itself off.
  • If nothing happens for 5 minutes, hold the power button. Your report is already saved up to that point. Then start the stick again and pick the normal scan. It remembers the step that froze, skips only that step, and finishes the rest.
  • If it freezes a second time, pick safe scan at the menu. It skips the graphics test.
4 · Put your settings back
  1. Turn the computer on and go into BIOS setup (usually Del or F2 as it starts). Set Secure Boot back to Enabled, then save and exit.
  2. Start Windows and open 1401 Probe. The Find my report page checks Secure Boot and BitLocker for you and offers to turn BitLocker back on.
  3. Your boot order was not changed. The boot menu is a one-time choice.

5 · Upload the report

  1. In 1401 Probe, click I already scanned - find my report, then Show both files.
  2. Click Open the upload page (nullmothsystems.com) and drag both files onto it: the .zip and the .txt with the same name, from the stick's REPORTS folder.
  3. It prints a report ID like NM-XXXXXXXX. Keep it: that's how we find your report if we have a question. That's it, and thank you.

What the scanner does and never does

  • Reads: processor, motherboard, BIOS/UEFI settings, graphics cards, Wi-Fi and Bluetooth chips, sound, USB ports, drive models and sizes, and firmware tables.
  • Writes: one report, to its own stick only.
  • Never connects to the internet. It has no network, Wi-Fi or Bluetooth drivers.
  • Never reads, opens or changes your drives. Every drive except its own stick is locked read-only before the scan starts.
  • Never changes BIOS/UEFI settings.
  • Removes serial numbers, UUIDs, MAC addresses and your Windows key from the report. Open the .zip and look. It's all plain text or raw firmware dumps.

Done with the stick? In 1401 Probe, Find my report, then Make it an ordinary USB drive again.
