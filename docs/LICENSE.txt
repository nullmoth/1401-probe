1. WHAT 1401 PROBE IS
   1401 Probe is a free tool from NullMoth Systems. It has two parts:
     - a Windows app that turns a USB stick into a hardware scanner, and
     - the scanner itself, a small offline system that starts from that stick, reads what hardware
       your computer has, and saves one report (a .zip file and a .txt summary) to the stick.

2. WHAT YOU MAY DO WITH IT

   as the GPL describes. The source code of every version NullMoth Systems publishes is at
   nullmothsystems.com/source.

3. WHAT IT DOES TO YOUR COMPUTER
   - The Windows app ERASES the USB stick you choose, after asking you twice. It only lists USB
     drives, never the drive Windows runs from.
   - The scanner never connects to the internet. It contains no network, Wi-Fi or Bluetooth drivers.
   - The scanner never reads, mounts or changes your internal drives. Before it scans anything it
     tells the system to treat every drive except its own stick as read-only.
   - The scanner never changes your BIOS/UEFI settings. You change Secure Boot yourself, and the
     app reminds you to turn it back on afterwards.
   - If you choose to, the app pauses BitLocker for 3 restarts. It resumes by itself, and the app
     offers to resume it straight away once you are done.

4. YOUR REPORT
   The report stays on your USB stick. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you upload it yourself at
   nullmothsystems.com. The scanner removes serial numbers, UUIDs, MAC addresses and your Windows
   product key before it saves the report. You can open the .zip and read everything in it before
   you send it. What happens to an uploaded report is described at nullmothsystems.com/privacy.
